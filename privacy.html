Privacy Policy

Sill for Mac · effective 1 October 2026

Sill collects no data. Nothing you write, paste or change in Sill is sent to me. It goes to other people only when you share it with them.

Where your notes live

Your notes, pictures and settings are stored on your Mac, inside Sill's App Sandbox container. There is no Sill account, no sign-in and no server of mine.

Sync with iCloud

When Sync is on (Settings → Sync, on unless you turn it off), your notes, Spaces, pictures and trash are also stored in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other Macs signed in to the same Apple Account. They go only to Apple's iCloud, under your Apple Account; I cannot see or read them, and neither can anyone else. Settings stay on each Mac. Turn Sync off and nothing is sent or fetched any more; what is already in iCloud stays there until you delete it, as with any app's iCloud data (System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud).

Sharing

When you share a note or a Space, it moves to its own place in your iCloud that the people you invite can open with their own Apple Account. They can read and change it, and so can anyone else you invited to it. Sill does not send invitations itself: it opens a ready message in your own Mail or Messages, or AirDrop, and you decide whether to send it.

Everyone in a share sees the names of the others in it, as Apple gives them, and while someone has a shared note open, the others see their name and the name of their Mac on that note. Names and email addresses of the people in your shares are shown only in Sill (Settings → People); they are not sent to me. You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time; someone who was invited can leave. A shared note can still be copied by anyone who could open it.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens an email in your own mail app, with the versions of Sill and macOS filled in. Nothing from your notes is added, and nothing is sent until you press Send.

What Sill does not do

  • It talks to nothing but iCloud, and only while Sync is on: your own iCloud, and the shares you were invited to. Pictures pasted from a web page are left out rather than fetched.
  • It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and no third-party code that does.
  • It does not read other apps' data, your screen or your keyboard outside its own panel.

Backups

If you turn on backups in Settings, Sill copies your notes to a folder you choose. Where that folder lives — on your Mac, an external drive or iCloud Drive — is your choice, and it is then handled by that service's own privacy terms.

Links you open

⌘-clicking a link in a note opens it in your default browser. What that website collects is covered by its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: github.com/dalee88/sill/issues.

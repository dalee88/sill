Privacy Policy

Sill for Mac · effective 28 September 2026

Sill collects no data. Nothing you write, paste or change in Sill is sent to me or to anyone else.

Where your notes live

Your notes, pictures and settings are stored only on your Mac, inside Sill's App Sandbox container. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

What Sill does not do

  • It makes no network requests of its own. Pictures pasted from a web page are left out rather than fetched.
  • It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and no third-party code that does.
  • It does not read other apps' data, your screen or your keyboard outside its own panel.

Backups

If you turn on backups in Settings, Sill copies your notes to a folder you choose. Where that folder lives — on your Mac, an external drive or iCloud Drive — is your choice, and it is then handled by that service's own privacy terms.

Links you open

⌘-clicking a link in a note opens it in your default browser. What that website collects is covered by its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: github.com/dalee88/sill/issues.

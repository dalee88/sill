Sill

A scratchpad a keystroke away.

Sill lives in your Mac's menu bar. One shortcut opens a small panel with the note you were writing; press Escape and you are back in the app you were using.

Getting started

  • Open Sill with Control+Option+N, or click the Sill icon in the menu bar. Right-click the icon for the menu with Quit.
  • Put it away with Escape, the same shortcut, or the menu-bar icon.
  • Change the shortcut, the look and the text size in Settings (⌘,).
  • Dock or float: the pin in the top bar hangs the panel under the menu-bar icon, or lets it float as a window of its own.

Your notes and backups

Notes are stored on your Mac, inside Sill's own sandbox. Deleted notes stay in Settings → Trash for 30 days. Settings → Backups copies everything to a folder you choose once a day; put that folder in iCloud Drive if you want your backups on another Mac too.

Questions, problems and ideas

Sill is made by one person, Daniel van Leest. Open an issue on github.com/dalee88/sill/issues and it reaches me directly. Please mention your macOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

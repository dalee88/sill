Sill

A scratchpad a keystroke away.

Sill lives in your Mac's menu bar. One shortcut opens a small panel with the note you were writing; press Escape and you are back in the app you were using.

Getting started

  • Open Sill with Control+Option+N, or click the Sill icon in the menu bar. Right-click the icon for the menu with Quit.
  • Put it away with Escape, the same shortcut, or the menu-bar icon.
  • Change the shortcut, the look and the text size in Settings (⌘,).
  • Dock or float: the pin in the top bar hangs the panel under the menu-bar icon, or lets it float as a window of its own.
  • Sheets: ⇧⌘N, or the grid button under the list, makes a note that is a small table: type numbers, =SUM(B2:B6) and other formulas, and style the cells.

On all your Macs

With Sync on (Settings → Sync), your notes, Spaces and pictures go to your own iCloud and appear on every Mac signed in to the same Apple Account, usually within seconds. Edits made on two Macs at once are merged, and Sill works offline: what you write waits until there is a connection.

Sharing

  • Invite someone in Settings → People, or with Share… in a note's or a Space's menu: their Apple Account email and what to share. Send the one link by Mail, Messages or AirDrop; when they open it on their Mac, everything you chose comes along.
  • Everyone invited can edit, and you see who is in a note right now: a small round mark with their initials at the top.
  • Remove someone, stop sharing or leave in Settings → People.
  • The people you invite need Sill on a Mac and an Apple Account.

Your notes and backups

Notes are stored on your Mac, inside Sill's own sandbox, and with Sync on in your own iCloud. Deleted notes stay in Settings → Trash for 30 days. Settings → Backups copies everything to a folder you choose once a day.
